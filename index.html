    Why your files stay private

    Most online PDF compressors send your document to their server, shrink it there and send it back. This one works differently. The compression engine is downloaded to your browser the first time you use it (about 15 MB, once), and from then on every file is processed on your own computer or phone.

    Nothing is uploaded, nothing is stored, and there is no account to create. That makes it safe for contracts, invoices, school reports and anything else you would not hand to a stranger.

    How to use it

    1. Pick a level. Balanced suits most files.
    2. Drop in one PDF or a whole batch.
    3. Check how much smaller each file is, then download it. Not small enough? Switch level and the files are redone for you.

    Questions people ask

    How much smaller will my PDF get?

    It depends on what is inside. Scans, photo-heavy brochures and presentations exported at full quality often shrink by 50 to 90 percent. PDFs that are mostly text are already small, so they may only lose a little, and the tool will tell you when there is nothing worth saving.

    Will it lower the quality?

    Text and vector drawings stay sharp at every level. The savings come from resizing and recompressing images. Light keeps images print-ready, Balanced keeps them clear on screens, and Maximum trades some image sharpness for the smallest file.

    Is there a file size limit?

    There is no limit set by us. The practical limit is your device's memory: computers handle files of several hundred megabytes, while phones are happier under 100 MB.

    Why does the first file take longer?

    The first time, your browser downloads the compression engine. After that it is kept in your browser's cache and files start right away.

    Can it compress password-protected PDFs?

    No. Remove the password in your PDF reader first, then optimise the unlocked copy.

    No. It is free to use, with no sign-up and no watermark on your files.
