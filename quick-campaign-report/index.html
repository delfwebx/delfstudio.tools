delfstudio. Quick Campaign Report

Turn tracking figures into a report your team will read.

For teams who send with Salesforce Marketing Cloud. Type in the figures from the tracking Overview tab and get a visual report that pastes cleanly into Outlook. Add more sends to compare them in one report. Everything you type stays in your browser.

1. Enter the figures

One block per send. Copy the numbers from the tracking Overview tab. Rates are worked out for you, and anything that does not add up is flagged.

2. Your report

Wording

The takeaways and next steps are written from your figures. Edit any line and the report updates.

How the status labels are set

Open and click rates follow the GEMS Education standard. Click-to-open rate follows the education industry median of about 7% (MailerLite benchmarks, December 2024 to November 2025). Delivery and unsubscribe rates follow general email norms.

MetricHow it is worked outLabels
