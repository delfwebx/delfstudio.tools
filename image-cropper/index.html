    How the queue works

    1. Add all your images at once. The first one opens in the cropper.
    2. Pick a size and corner style, then drag the frame to choose what stays in the picture.
    3. Press Crop and save. The next image opens with the same settings, so you only need to reposition the frame.
    4. Download each result as it is ready, or all of them as one ZIP at the end.

    Why your images stay private

    Cropping and compressing happen on your own computer or phone, inside the browser. Nothing is uploaded and nothing is stored. The saved copies also leave out the hidden details cameras attach to photos, such as where a picture was taken.

    Questions people ask

    How do I make a round profile picture?

    Choose Circle under Corners, drag the frame over the face, and save. Pick PNG or WebP to keep the area outside the circle see-through, or JPEG to fill it with a colour of your choice.

    Why are circle and rounded crops saved as PNG?

    The corners outside the shape need to be see-through, and JPEG cannot do that. PNG can and opens everywhere, including email. WebP can too and makes smaller files, but some older email programs do not show it.

    Are the social media sizes up to date?

    They follow the sizes the platforms recommended when this tool was last updated. Platforms change them from time to time, so for anything important check the platform's own help page, or use Your own size to enter the numbers yourself.

    What happens if my crop is smaller than the size I picked?

    The picture is enlarged to reach the exact size, which can make it look soft. The cropper warns you when that is about to happen so you can choose a larger area or a bigger photo.

    Can I change a crop after saving it?

    Yes. Press Edit crop next to any image in the list, adjust the frame, and save again. The new version replaces the old one.
