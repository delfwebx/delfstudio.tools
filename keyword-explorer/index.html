Good to know

  • Your login stays with you. It is kept in this browser and sent only to api.dataforseo.com. Leave "Remember" off on a shared computer, and it is forgotten when you close the tab.
  • Numbers differ between tools. Ahrefs, Semrush and DataForSEO each estimate volume their own way, so compare keywords within one tool rather than across tools.
  • Difficulty runs from 0 to 100. It reflects how strong the pages already ranking are. Under 30 is usually realistic for a newer site.
  • Autocomplete is a good free signal. If Google suggests a phrase, real people are typing it. It just does not tell you how many.
  • If autocomplete stops answering, the search engine may be limiting quick repeat requests. Wait a minute and try again.
